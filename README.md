# WedPlan – शुभ विवाह

**A wedding planner made for Nepali weddings.**
Plan every ceremony, ritual, guest, purchase and booking in one place – on your phone or computer.

👉 **Open WedPlan:** https://rachusharma.github.io/WedPlan/

No sign-up, no download. Just open the link.

---

## Getting started

1. **Open the link** on your phone or computer.
2. **Tell WedPlan about your wedding:** the couple's names, the wedding date, the place and (if you like) your budget.
   The date is also shown in Bikram Sambat, e.g. *8 Falgun 2083 BS*.
3. **Choose your side:** are you planning for the bride's family or the groom's family?
4. **Add family members** (optional) – so you can give them jobs later.
5. Tap **Save and continue**. You're on your **Overview**.

> 💡 **Tip:** on your phone, add WedPlan to your home screen so it opens like an app.
> iPhone: Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Android: menu ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*.

## The three first steps

The **Overview** shows a small "Start here" guide:

1. **Choose your ceremonies** – Tika Talo, Swayamvar, Kanyadaan, Bidaai and more. Each one comes with its traditional rituals to tick off.
2. **Add your guests** – start with close family, add the rest little by little.
3. **Share out the work** – give family members jobs: buying, booking, looking after the guests or the pandit's puja items.

## What you can do

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| 🏠 **Overview** | Countdown, your progress, what needs attention today, and whether you're on track. |
| 🪔 **Ceremonies** | Every ceremony with date, place and its rituals. After a ceremony, write down a *moment to remember*. |
| ✅ **Tasks** | Your to-do list with due dates and who is doing it. |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 **Guests** | Who is invited, from which side, and who has confirmed. |
| 💰 **Budget** | Your total budget, what's planned and what's paid. Shopping and booking costs are added automatically. |
| 🛍️ **Shopping** | Things to buy for each occasion, from outfits to puja samagri. |
| 📅 **Bookings** | Venue, pandit, Naumati Baja, makeup artist, catering… |
| 🙋 **Helpers** | Family and friends who take care of things on the day. |
| 👤 **My details** | Edit your wedding details and family, and save or load your plan. |

## Working together with family

- Give each item a **responsible person** (e.g. *Didi – buy the tika saree*).
- If nothing happens for a while, tap **Remind** to send a friendly reminder on **WhatsApp**.
- When it's done, tap **Say thanks 🙏** to send a thank-you.
- The 🔔 bell shows what changed and what needs attention.

## Good to know

- **Removed something by mistake?** Tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen.
- **Not sure where to start?** Tap *Suggestions* on any list – WedPlan suggests common items for Nepali weddings.

## Your data and privacy

- Your plan is saved **only on your own phone or computer**, in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
- Each phone has its **own** plan. Family members on other phones won't see your changes automatically.
- ⚠️ **Clearing your browser data or using a private window will delete your plan.**

### Keep your plan safe / move it to another phone

1. Go to **My details → Download backup**. A file is saved on your device.
2. Keep it somewhere safe, or send it to yourself or family (e.g. on WhatsApp).
3. On the new phone, open WedPlan and tap **Load a backup** on the first screen.
   Your whole plan comes back.
